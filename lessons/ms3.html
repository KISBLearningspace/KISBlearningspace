Mock Paper 3: Level 3 (Grades 10–12)

1IB Syllabus Link & Learning Outcomes

Purpose: A timed practice paper for olympiad-style competitions, for Grades 10–12. All 25 questions are original. They use the tricks from the Olympiad lessons: testing options, patterns, algebra identities, number theory, counting, inequalities and geometry.
How to use
Find a quiet place, set 60 minutes, press Start timer and work without a calculator. Choose an answer for every question. Then press Check my answers. Open the solutions at the end of the page and mark which trick you could have used.

Guiding questions

  • Which questions give the most marks for the least time?
  • Which trick would have saved me time on each question I missed?
  • How can I plan my 60 minutes?

Learning outcomes

  • Practise working under time pressure.
  • Choose the best trick for each question.
  • Check answers and learn from the solutions.
  • Plan time: A about 20 minutes, B about 25 minutes, C about 15 minutes.
  • Record the trick you used for every question.

2Study Notes

Time plan. Section A (10 questions, 3 marks): about 20 minutes. Section B (10 questions, 4 marks): about 25 minutes. Section C (5 questions, 6 marks): about 15 minutes. Skip a question after 3 minutes and return later.

10Exam-style Questions

Answers and solutions. Open each solution after you have tried the question.

Question 1 [3 marks] Answer: (A) 17
Solutionm² + n² = (m + n)² − 2mn = 25 − 8 = 17.
Question 2 [3 marks] Answer: (D) 4
Solution3 + 2 − 1 = 4.
Question 3 [3 marks] Answer: (C) 1425
Solutiona = 4, d = 3. The 30th term is 4 + 29 × 3 = 91. Sum = ⟦30|2⟧(4 + 91) = 1425.
Question 4 [3 marks] Answer: (E) 8
Solution−9 < 2n − 5 < 9 gives −2 < n < 7, so n = −1, 0, 1, …, 6: that is 8 integers.
Question 5 [3 marks] Answer: (B) 2
Solution⟦1|2⟧ + ⟦1|2⟧ + 1 = 2.
Question 6 [3 marks] Answer: (B) ⟦5|36⟧
SolutionThe pairs (2, 6), (3, 5), (4, 4), (5, 3), (6, 2) give 5 of 36 outcomes.
Question 7 [3 marks] Answer: (E) −1
Solutionx² = 2x + k has one solution: x² − 2x − k = 0 has discriminant 4 + 4k = 0, so k = −1.
Question 8 [3 marks] Answer: (D) 15
SolutionC(6, 2) = ⟦6 × 5|2⟧ = 15.
Question 9 [3 marks] Answer: (C) 5
Solutiona² − 3a − 10 = 0 gives (a − 5)(a + 2) = 0. As a > 0, a = 5.
Question 10 [3 marks] Answer: (A) 6560
SolutionUse differences of squares: (3² − 1)(3² + 1) = 3⁴ − 1, then (3⁴ − 1)(3⁴ + 1) = 3⁸ − 1 = 6560.
Question 11 [4 marks] Answer: (E) 527
Solutionx² + ⟦1|x²⟧ = 25 − 2 = 23. Then x⁴ + ⟦1|x⁴⟧ = 23² − 2 = 527.
Question 12 [4 marks] Answer: (C) ⟦1|2⟧
Solution⟦1|a⟧ + ⟦1|b⟧ = ⟦8|ab⟧ and ab ≤ 16 (AM-GM, equality at a = b = 4). So the minimum is ⟦8|16⟧ = ⟦1|2⟧.
Question 13 [4 marks] Answer: (A) 4
SolutionA divisor is a square when every exponent is even: 2 has {0, 2}, 3 has {0, 2}, 5 has {0}, 7 has {0}. So 2 × 2 = 4 (1, 4, 9, 36).
Question 14 [4 marks] Answer: (D) 31
Solution⌊⟦125|5⟧⌋ + ⌊⟦125|25⟧⌋ + ⌊⟦125|125⟧⌋ = 25 + 5 + 1 = 31.
Question 15 [4 marks] Answer: (B) 35
SolutionPlace 6 empty seats; they make 7 gaps. Choose 4 gaps: C(7, 4) = 35.
Question 16 [4 marks] Answer: (D) 68°
Solution∠AOB = 180° − 44° = 136° (angles of quadrilateral OAPB). The angle at the circle is half: 68°.
Question 17 [4 marks] Answer: (B) 30
SolutionTriangle BCE has half the area of ABC: 45. D is ⟦2|3⟧ of the way along BC, so area BDE = ⟦2|3⟧ × 45 = 30.
Question 18 [4 marks] Answer: (E) 3
SolutionGeometric series with first term 1 and common ratio ⟦2|3⟧. The sum is 1 ÷ (1 − ⟦2|3⟧) = 1 ÷ ⟦1|3⟧ = 3.
Question 19 [4 marks] Answer: (C) 1
Solution3⁶ ≡ 1 and 100 = 6 × 16 + 4, so 3¹⁰⁰ ≡ 3⁴ = 81 ≡ 4. 4³ ≡ 1 and 100 = 3 × 33 + 1, so 4¹⁰⁰ ≡ 4. Sum ≡ 8 ≡ 1.
Question 20 [4 marks] Answer: (A) ⟦7|15⟧
SolutionSame colour: C(4, 2) + C(6, 2) = 6 + 15 = 21 out of C(10, 2) = 45, which is ⟦21|45⟧ = ⟦7|15⟧.
Question 21 [6 marks] Answer: 48
Solution(a − b)(a + b) = 77 = 1 × 77 or 7 × 11. These give a = 39 and a = 9. Sum = 48.
Question 22 [6 marks] Answer: 25
SolutionLet each be 1 to 6 (like three dice summing to 12). The sums of 12 occur in 25 of the 216 outcomes: 6-5-1 (6 ways), 6-4-2 (6), 6-3-3 (3), 5-5-2 (3), 5-4-3 (6), 4-4-4 (1). Total 6 + 6 + 3 + 3 + 6 + 1 = 25.
Question 23 [6 marks] Answer: 62
SolutionS₁₀ = 5 × (10 + 9d) = 185 gives 10 + 9d = 37, so d = 3. The 20th term is 5 + 19 × 3 = 62.
Question 24 [6 marks] Answer: 49
Solution7⁴ = 2401, which ends in 01. 2026 = 4 × 506 + 2, so 7²⁰²⁶ ends in the same two digits as 7² = 49.
Question 25 [6 marks] Answer: 4
SolutionSemi-perimeter s = 21. By Heron’s formula the area is √{21 × 8 × 7 × 6} = 84. The radius is area ÷ s = 84 ÷ 21 = 4.
