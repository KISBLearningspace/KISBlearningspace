2Study Notes

Radian. One radian is the angle at the centre of a circle when the arc length equals the radius. Since the whole circumference is 2πr, a full turn is 2π radians.
π rad = 180°   so   1 rad = ⟦180°|π⟧ ≈ 57.3°
To convert: degrees → radians multiply by ⟦π|180⟧; radians → degrees multiply by ⟦180|π⟧.
Degrees0°30°45°60°90°180°270°360°
Radians0⟦π|6⟧⟦π|4⟧⟦π|3⟧⟦π|2⟧π⟦3π|2⟧2π
Arc, sector and segment (angle θ in radians, radius r):
arc length l = rθ
sector area A = ⟦1|2⟧r²θ
segment area = ⟦1|2⟧r²(θ − sin θ)
If θ is in degrees, use l = ⟦θ|360⟧ × 2πr and A = ⟦θ|360⟧ × πr². These are in the formula booklet.
Example 1 (convert). Convert 150° to radians and ⟦3π|4⟧ to degrees.
  1. Key fact. 180° = π radians.
  2. Degrees to radians: multiply by ⟦π|180⟧. 150 × ⟦π|180⟧ = ⟦150π|180⟧ = ⟦5π|6⟧.
  3. Radians to degrees: multiply by ⟦180|π⟧. ⟦3π|4⟧ × ⟦180|π⟧ = ⟦3 × 180|4⟧ = 135°.
Answer: 150° = ⟦5π|6⟧ rad; ⟦3π|4⟧ rad = 135°.
Example 2 (arc and sector). r = 6 and θ = ⟦2π|3⟧ (radians). Find the arc length and the sector area.
  1. Formulas (θ in radians). Arc l = rθ; area A = ⟦1|2⟧r²θ.
  2. Arc length. l = 6 × ⟦2π|3⟧ = 4π ≈ 12.6.
  3. Sector area. A = ⟦1|2⟧(36)(⟦2π|3⟧) = 18 × ⟦2π|3⟧ = 12π ≈ 37.7.
Answer: Arc 4π ≈ 12.6; area 12π ≈ 37.7.
Example 3 (find the angle). A sector has radius 4 and arc length 10. Find θ.
  1. Use l = rθ. 10 = 4θ.
  2. Solve. θ = ⟦10|4⟧ = 2.5 rad.
  3. Convert (optional). 2.5 × ⟦180|π⟧ ≈ 143°.
Answer: θ = 2.5 rad ≈ 143°
Example 4 (segment). r = 10 and θ = 1.2 rad. Find the area of the segment and the length of the chord.
  1. The segment is the sector minus the triangle. Sector = ⟦1|2⟧r²θ and triangle = ⟦1|2⟧r² sin θ.
  2. Segment area. ⟦1|2⟧r²(θ − sin θ) = ⟦1|2⟧(100)(1.2 − sin 1.2) = 50(1.2 − 0.9320) ≈ 13.4.
  3. Chord (from the isosceles triangle, using half the angle). Chord = 2r sin ⟦θ|2⟧ = 20 sin 0.6 ≈ 11.3.
  4. Remember the calculator mode. Use radians for these calculations.
Answer: Segment area ≈ 13.4; chord ≈ 11.3
Example 5 (perimeter). A sector has perimeter 20 and radius 5. Find θ and the area.
  1. Perimeter = two radii + arc. 20 = 5 + 5 + arc, so the arc = 10.
  2. Find θ. l = rθ, so 10 = 5θ and θ = 2 rad.
  3. Find the area. A = ⟦1|2⟧r²θ = ⟦1|2⟧(25)(2) = 25.
Answer: θ = 2 rad; area = 25
Think: ① Why does the arc length formula l = rθ only work with θ in radians? ② What is the angle in radians subtended by an arc equal to the radius? ③ For a fixed perimeter, what angle gives the largest sector area?

3History & Facts

  • Babylonians (about 2000 BCE): chose 360 degrees, possibly because their number system used base 60 and a year has about 360 days.
  • Roger Cotes (1714): described the radian idea; the word “radian” was first printed by James Thomson in 1873.
  • Euler: showed that radians make calculus formulas simple, for example the derivative of sin x is cos x only when x is in radians.
  • Fact: the angle of the Earth’s motion in its orbit is about ⟦2π|365⟧ radians per day.

5Real-World Applications

  • Engineering: gear teeth, pulleys and the length of a belt around wheels.
  • Astronomy: angular size of the Moon (about 0.0089 rad) and distance by arc length.
  • Design and architecture: arches, curved roofs and circular windows.
  • Navigation: distance along a line of latitude or longitude is arc length on the Earth.
  • Physics: angular velocity ω = ⟦θ|t⟧ in rad/s, and the speed v = rω.

7Classwork & Homework

Classwork

  1. Convert 72° to radians and ⟦7π|6⟧ to degrees.
    Answer72° = ⟦2π|5⟧ rad. ⟦7π|6⟧ = 210°.
  2. A sector has radius 8 cm and angle 0.9 rad. Find the arc length and area.
    AnswerArc = 7.2 cm. Area = ⟦1|2⟧(64)(0.9) = 28.8 cm².
  3. A sector has radius 5 and area 20. Find the angle and the arc length.
    Answer⟦1|2⟧(25)θ = 20, θ = 1.6 rad. Arc = 8.
  4. Find the perimeter of a sector with radius 6 and angle ⟦π|3⟧ in exact form.
    Answer12 + 6(⟦π|3⟧) = 12 + 2π.

Homework

  1. Find the exact area of a sector with radius 12 and angle 150°.
    Answer150° = ⟦5π|6⟧. Area = ⟦1|2⟧(144)(⟦5π|6⟧) = 60π.
  2. A sector of radius 10 has arc length 25. Find the angle in radians and degrees.
    Answerθ = 2.5 rad = 143.2°.
  3. Find the area of the segment for r = 9 and θ = 1.5 rad.
    Answer⟦1|2⟧(81)(1.5 − sin 1.5) = 40.5(1.5 − 0.99749) ≈ 20.4.
  4. A sector has perimeter 24 and radius 6. Show that its angle is 2 rad and find its area.
    AnswerArc = 24 − 12 = 12, θ = ⟦12|6⟧ = 2. Area = ⟦1|2⟧(36)(2) = 36.

Marks: M = method, A = accuracy, R = reasoning. Open “Mark scheme” only after trying.

Paper 1 style (no calculator)

Question 1 [5 marks] A sector OAB has radius 6 cm and angle ⟦π|3⟧ radians. Find, in terms of π:
(a) the arc length AB [1]
(b) the area of the sector [2]
(c) the perimeter of the sector [2]
Mark scheme(a) A1 2π cm.
(b) M1 ⟦1|2⟧(36)(⟦π|3⟧); A1 6π cm².
(c) M1 12 + 2π; A1 correct statement (12 + 2π) cm.
Question 2 [5 marks] A sector of a circle with radius 6 has area 12π.
(a) Find the angle at the centre. [2]
(b) Find the arc length. [1]
(c) Write the angle in degrees. [2]
Mark scheme(a) M1 ⟦1|2⟧(36)θ = 12π; A1 θ = ⟦2π|3⟧.
(b) A1 arc = 4π.
(c) M1 ⟦2π|3⟧ × ⟦180|π⟧; A1 120°.

Paper 2 style (GDC allowed)

Question 3 [8 marks] The diagram shows a sector OAB of a circle with centre O, radius 12 cm and angle AOB = 1.4 radians.
(a) Find the length of arc AB. [2]
(b) Find the area of the sector. [2]
(c) Find the length of the chord AB. [2]
(d) Find the area of the shaded segment between the chord and the arc. [2]
