Guiding questions

  • Why does the ratio of two sides depend only on the angle, not on the size of the triangle?
  • How can an angle be found from two lengths?
  • Why do 30°, 45° and 60° have exact values?

Learning outcomes

  • Label the hypotenuse, opposite and adjacent sides for a given angle.
  • Use sin, cos and tan to find a missing side.
  • Use the inverse functions to find a missing angle.
  • Recall the exact values for 30°, 45° and 60°.
  • Use sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 for an acute angle.

2Study Notes

The three ratios. In a right-angled triangle, for an acute angle θ:
sin θ = ⟦opposite|hypotenuse⟧   cos θ = ⟦adjacent|hypotenuse⟧   tan θ = ⟦opposite|adjacent⟧
Memory aid: SOH CAH TOA. The sides are named from the point of view of θ. The hypotenuse is always opposite the right angle.
Method. ① Draw and label the triangle. ② Mark the known side and the wanted side as O, A or H. ③ Choose the ratio that uses exactly those two. ④ Solve. Set the calculator to degrees. To find an angle use sin−1, cos−1 or tan−1.
Example 1 (find a side). Hypotenuse 12 and angle 35°. Find the opposite and adjacent sides.
  1. Label the triangle. Hypotenuse H = 12; relative to the 35° angle, the opposite is O and the adjacent is A.
  2. Choose the ratio linking O and H. sin θ = ⟦O|H⟧, so O = 12 sin 35° ≈ 6.88.
  3. Choose the ratio linking A and H. cos θ = ⟦A|H⟧, so A = 12 cos 35° ≈ 9.83.
  4. Check. 6.88² + 9.83² ≈ 144 = 12² ✓
Answer: Opposite ≈ 6.88; adjacent ≈ 9.83.
Example 2 (find an angle). Opposite 5 and adjacent 8. Find θ.
  1. Choose the ratio. We know O and A, so use tan θ = ⟦O|A⟧.
  2. Substitute. tan θ = ⟦5|8⟧.
  3. Use the inverse function. θ = tan−1(⟦5|8⟧) ≈ 32.0°.
Answer: θ ≈ 32.0°
Example 3 (unknown in the denominator). Adjacent side 7 and θ = 40°. Find the hypotenuse x.
  1. Choose the ratio. We know A and want H: cos θ = ⟦A|H⟧.
  2. Substitute. cos 40° = ⟦7|x⟧.
  3. Rearrange. x cos 40° = 7, so x = ⟦7|cos 40°⟧.
  4. Calculate. x ≈ ⟦7|0.766⟧ ≈ 9.14.
Answer: x ≈ 9.14

30°⟦1|2⟧⟦√{3}|2⟧⟦1|√{3}⟧

Where do the special values come from? Find sin, cos, tan of 30°, 60° and 45° exactly.
  1. Half of an equilateral triangle. Take an equilateral triangle of side 2 and cut it in half. The right-angled triangle has sides 1, √{3}, 2 and angles 30°, 60°, 90° (the height is √{2² − 1²} = √{3}).
  2. Read off the ratios. sin 30° = ⟦1|2⟧, cos 30° = ⟦√{3}|2⟧, tan 30° = ⟦1|√{3}⟧; sin 60° = ⟦√{3}|2⟧, cos 60° = ⟦1|2⟧, tan 60° = √{3}.
  3. Isosceles right triangle. Equal sides 1 give hypotenuse √{2} and two 45° angles. So sin 45° = cos 45° = ⟦1|√{2}⟧ and tan 45° = 1.
Answer: Use these exact values without a calculator.
Example 4 (given one ratio). θ is acute and sin θ = ⟦3|5⟧. Find cos θ and tan θ.
  1. Draw a right triangle. sin θ = ⟦O|H⟧, so take O = 3 and H = 5.
  2. Find the missing side with Pythagoras. A = √{5² − 3²} = √{16} = 4.
  3. Read the other ratios. cos θ = ⟦A|H⟧ = ⟦4|5⟧ and tan θ = ⟦O|A⟧ = ⟦3|4⟧.
Answer: cos θ = ⟦4|5⟧, tan θ = ⟦3|4⟧
Example 5 (ladder). A 5 m ladder makes 70° with the ground. How high does it reach and how far is its foot from the wall?
  1. Draw the triangle. The ladder is the hypotenuse H = 5; the wall height is opposite the 70° angle.
  2. Height. sin 70° = ⟦height|5⟧, so height = 5 sin 70° ≈ 4.70 m.
  3. Distance from the wall (adjacent). cos 70° = ⟦distance|5⟧, so distance = 5 cos 70° ≈ 1.71 m.
Answer: It reaches about 4.70 m; the foot is about 1.71 m from the wall.
Identities for acute θ: tan θ = ⟦sin θ|cos θ⟧ and sin²θ + cos²θ = 1. The second is Pythagoras in a triangle with hypotenuse 1.
Think: ① If two right-angled triangles have the same acute angle, what is the same about them? ② Why can sin θ never be more than 1 for an acute angle? ③ Why does tan θ get very large as θ approaches 90°?

3History & Facts

  • Hipparchus (about 150 BCE): built the first table of chords, often called the father of trigonometry.
  • Indian astronomers (about 500 CE): Aryabhata used a half-chord (“jya”), which became the sine. The word passed through Arabic into Latin as “sinus”.
  • Al-Battani and Islamic mathematicians (900s): used sine, cosine and tangent in astronomy and found the direction of Mecca.
  • Surveying: triangulation, measuring a baseline and angles, was used to map whole countries.

5Real-World Applications

  • Surveying and mapping: heights of mountains and widths of rivers from angles and a baseline.
  • Construction: roof pitch, ramp slope and stair angles.
  • Navigation and aviation: glide angle on approach to an airport.
  • Astronomy: distances to nearby stars from parallax angles.
  • Physics: resolving a force or velocity into components.

Classwork

  1. Find the side marked x: hypotenuse 15, angle 28°, x is opposite the angle.
    Answerx = 15 sin 28° ≈ 7.04.
  2. Find the angle: opposite 9, hypotenuse 14.
    Answersin θ = ⟦9|14⟧, θ ≈ 40.0°.
  3. θ is acute and cos θ = ⟦5|13⟧. Find sin θ and tan θ.
    AnswerOpposite = √{169 − 25} = 12. sin θ = ⟦12|13⟧, tan θ = ⟦12|5⟧.
  4. Find the exact value of 8 sin 60° − 4 cos 30°.
    Answer8(⟦√{3}|2⟧) − 4(⟦√{3}|2⟧) = 4√{3} − 2√{3} = 2√{3}.

Homework

  1. A ramp of length 6 m rises 1.2 m. Find its angle with the ground.
    Answersin θ = ⟦1.2|6⟧ = 0.2; θ ≈ 11.5°.
  2. Find x: adjacent side 10, angle 52°, x is opposite.
    Answerx = 10 tan 52° ≈ 12.8.
  3. Show that tan 60° − tan 30° = ⟦2√{3}|3⟧.
    Answer√{3} − ⟦1|√{3}⟧ = ⟦3 − 1|√{3}⟧ = ⟦2|√{3}⟧ = ⟦2√{3}|3⟧.
  4. A right-angled triangle has hypotenuse 11 and one side 6. Find the other side and the smaller angle.
    AnswerOther side = √{121 − 36} = √{85} ≈ 9.22. The angle opposite 6: sin θ = ⟦6|11⟧, θ ≈ 33.1°.

10Exam-style Questions

Marks: M = method, A = accuracy, R = reasoning. Open “Mark scheme” only after trying.

Paper 1 style (no calculator)

Question 1 [5 marks] θ is an acute angle with sin θ = ⟦5|13⟧.
(a) Find cos θ. [3]
(b) Find tan θ. [1]
(c) Hence write down tan(90° − θ) in terms of θ’s triangle. [1]
Mark scheme(a) M1 triangle O = 5, H = 13; A1 A = 12; A1 cos θ = ⟦12|13⟧.
(b) A1 tan θ = ⟦5|12⟧.
(c) R1 tan(90° − θ) = ⟦12|5⟧ (the other acute angle swaps opposite and adjacent).
Question 2 [6 marks] A right-angled triangle has an angle of 60° and hypotenuse 8.
(a) Find the two shorter sides, in exact form. [3]
(b) Find the exact area. [3]
Mark scheme(a) M1 8 sin 60° and 8 cos 60°; A1 4√{3}; A1 4.
(b) M1 ⟦1|2⟧ × 4 × 4√{3}; A1 8√{3}; A1 correct form (≈ 13.9).

Paper 2 style (GDC allowed)

Question 3 [7 marks] A wheelchair ramp is 6 m long and rises 1.2 m above the ground.
(a) Find the angle the ramp makes with the ground. [2]
(b) Find the horizontal distance covered by the ramp. [2]
(c) A rule says the angle must not exceed 4.8°. Find the shortest ramp length that rises 1.2 m and meets the rule. [3]
Mark scheme(a) M1 sin θ = ⟦1.2|6⟧; A1 11.5°.
(b) M1 6 cos 11.54° or √{36 − 1.44}; A1 5.88 m.
(c) M1 sin 4.8° = ⟦1.2|L⟧; A1 L = ⟦1.2|sin 4.8°⟧; A1 14.3 m.
